A reminder text is table stakes. Practices that grow treat engagement as a product: booking, records, education, and feedback under the clinic’s own brand.

## Let patients self-serve the obvious

Book, reschedule, and pull records without calling reception. Every avoided phone tag is a calmer front desk and a happier patient.

## Meet them on WhatsApp and email

Patients already live in those apps. Use them for reminders, prep instructions, and follow-ups — with templates your team can trust.

## Feedback while the visit is fresh

Automated post-visit surveys catch service gaps early. Read them beside operational reports so praise and complaints both drive change.

## Education that belongs to your clinic

Condition guides and prep notes under your brand build trust better than generic links. Tie them to the visit type when you can.
