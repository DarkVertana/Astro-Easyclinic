## FAQ (keep live honesty)
1. **Which CMS should a private clinic in Kenya choose in 2026?**  
   For most private clinics: EasyClinic as all-rounder (Cura AI, native M-Pesa, transparent pricing, SHA/SHIF in development). Public-sector programme facilities: KenyaEMR/OpenMRS still often fit better.
2. **SHA / SHIF compliant today?**  
   Not yet. SHA, SHIF, and KRA eTIMS are in active development — eligibility, attachments, and submit-from-EMR are the aim.
3. **Best EMR for M-Pesa?**  
   EasyClinic — M-Pesa is part of billing, not a bolt-on; payments reconcile to invoices.
4. **Unreliable internet?**  
   Cloud-tuned for low bandwidth (incl. 3G outside Nairobi). Not fully offline today; self-hosted options discussed for larger groups in demo.
5. **Migration time?**  
   Most clinics 2–4 weeks (data import, training, SHA workflow prep). Multi-branch often 4–6. Dedicated implementation specialist.
6. **Monthly cost?**  
   See Pricing; demo for Kenya quote by doctors/branches/modules.
7. **Better than Helium for a Nairobi clinic?**  
   For ~1–10 branches, usually yes (pricing clarity, live AI, M-Pesa, SHA-in-core roadmap). Large hospital groups may still prefer Helium’s breadth.
8. **WhatsApp reminders?**  
   Yes — WhatsApp, SMS, and email. WhatsApp is what patients here actually read.
