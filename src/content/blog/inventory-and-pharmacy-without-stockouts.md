Stockouts rarely surprise the store room on Monday. They show up Friday afternoon when the popular SKU hits zero and nobody updated the reorder level.

## One catalogue from purchase to dispense

Purchase orders, transfers, and pharmacy sales should share the same item master. Duplicate lists are how “we have it upstairs” becomes “we ordered the wrong pack size.”

## Expiry flagged at the counter

Catching short-dated stock at sale protects patients and margins. It also trains the team to rotate inventory without a separate audit day.

## AI-assisted reorder levels

Historical consumption beats gut feel. EasyClinic inventory can suggest reorder points so busy weeks do not empty the shelf.

## Refills that patients remember

When the prescription flows to dispensing, refill reminders can go out without a second spreadsheet. Engagement and pharmacy stay connected.
