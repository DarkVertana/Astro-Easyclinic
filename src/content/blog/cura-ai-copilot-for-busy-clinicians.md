Clinic AI only helps if it shows up in the visit, not in a separate dashboard nobody opens. CuraPilot is the intelligence layer inside EasyClinic — clinical, operational, and financial signals from the same data.

## Advisory, not autonomous

Recommendations for protocols, investigations, and referrals are suggestions. The clinician reviews, edits, and signs. That boundary is the point.

## Safety before the script is printed

Drug interactions, allergy flags, and dose checks matter most when they interrupt a hurried prescribe — quietly and in context.

## Ops and claims in the same layer

Supply forecasts and claim pre-checks use the same visit data. That is how AI stops being a demo and starts protecting revenue and stock.

## Peer-reviewed where it counts

Cura has been studied in real deployments — including peer-reviewed work across tens of thousands of visits. Marketing claims are easy; measured outcomes are rarer.
