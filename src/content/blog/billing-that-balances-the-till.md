Clinic revenue leaks are rarely dramatic. They are missed part payments, tax rules applied by memory, and a till that only balances after an hour of spreadsheet archaeology.

## Invoice from the visit, not from memory

When the consult closes, the bill should already know the procedures, packages, and pharmacy lines. One click beats re-keying.

## Part payments without sticky notes

Deposits, insurance portions, and patient balances need a clear ledger. Staff should see what is owed without opening three screens.

## Cash, card, and online in one till

End-of-day reconciliation fails when channels live in different apps. A unified till view is the difference between closing on time and staying late.

## Tax and payer rules as configuration

Local tax and claim rules change. Encode them once so every invoice follows the same path — and Cura can pre-check claims before submission.
