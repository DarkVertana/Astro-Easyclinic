Empty slots hurt more than a full waiting room. Most no-shows are not patient indifference — they are forgotten appointments, unclear timing, or a booking flow that only staff understand.

## Make the calendar honest

Separate walk-in capacity from timed consults. Show teleconsults in the same day view so nobody double-books a room and a Zoom link.

## Remind on the channels patients already open

WhatsApp, SMS, and email reminders land better than a single call from the front desk. Send a confirm link, not just a time stamp.

## Waiting-room status that front desk trusts

When status moves from booked to arrived to with doctor, the queue stays calm. Patients see progress; staff stop hunting for who is next.

## Multi-location without five calendars

Chains need one patient across sites. A shared schedule with location-aware slots stops the “we thought you were at the other branch” calls.
